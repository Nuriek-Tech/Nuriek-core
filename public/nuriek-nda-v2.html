NURIEK
Non-Disclosure Agreement
Confidentiality & Proprietary Information Protection
VERSION 2.0

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of electronic signature below (the "Effective Date") by and between:

Disclosing Party Nuriek Inc., a company incorporated under applicable laws, having its registered office at Bangalore, Karnataka, India ("Company")
Receiving Party The individual whose details are registered in the Nuriek HR Portal at the time of execution ("Employee / Intern / Contractor")

Both parties are collectively referred to as the "Parties". In consideration of the mutual covenants and confidential information to be disclosed, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by Nuriek to the Receiving Party, including but not limited to:

  1. Business strategies, plans, financial projections, and forecasts;
  2. Source code, software architecture, APIs, databases, and technical documentation;
  3. Customer data, client lists, contracts, and pricing information;
  4. Product roadmaps, unreleased features, and research and development;
  5. Employee records, compensation structures, and HR policies;
  6. Any information marked as "Confidential" or which a reasonable person would consider confidential.

2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

  1. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and use at least the same degree of care as it uses for its own confidential information (but no less than reasonable care);
  2. Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent from Nuriek;
  3. Use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing obligations under their employment or engagement with Nuriek;
  4. Promptly notify Nuriek upon becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or misuse of Confidential Information;
  5. Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon termination of engagement with Nuriek.

3. Exclusions

This Agreement does not apply to information that:

  1. Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement;
  2. Was rightfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure by Nuriek;
  3. Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information;
  4. Is required to be disclosed by applicable law or court order, provided the Receiving Party gives Nuriek prompt written notice to seek a protective order.

4. Intellectual Property

All work product, inventions, improvements, software, designs, and developments created by the Receiving Party in the course of their engagement with Nuriek shall be deemed "Work Made for Hire" and shall be the exclusive property of Nuriek. The Receiving Party hereby assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work product to Nuriek.

5. Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date, or two (2) years after the termination of the Receiving Party's engagement with Nuriek, whichever is later. Obligations relating to trade secrets shall survive indefinitely.

6. Non-Solicitation

During the term of engagement and for a period of one (1) year thereafter, the Receiving Party agrees not to directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or hire any employee, contractor, or client of Nuriek.

7. Remedies

The Receiving Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to Nuriek for which monetary damages may be an insufficient remedy. Nuriek shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including injunctions and specific performance, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Bangalore, Karnataka.

9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements. This Agreement may only be modified in writing signed by both Parties.
